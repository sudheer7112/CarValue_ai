# Evaluator notes

The product is intentionally buyer-facing. It focuses on:
- fair-value band
- buying guidance
- vehicle signals
- engine/power/mileage details
- negotiation plan
- depreciation planning
- alternatives
- comparison

The app does not claim to certify vehicle safety and does not display a confidence score.

The technical training comparison is retained in `models/metrics.json` and `backend/train.py`
for evaluator questions, but the buyer UI does not expose a model-lab dashboard.
